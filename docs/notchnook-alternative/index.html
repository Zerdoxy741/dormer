The seven things only Dormer does

A pixel companion

A cat (or dog, bunny, robot, ghost) lives in your notch. Feed it, level it up, play hide-and-seek, throw it a ball. You can turn it off.

Listen together

Friends nearby see your song in their notch and listen in sync, with Spotify or Apple Music. No server, no account.

Reply from the notch

A message lands in the notch: type your answer right there and send it, without opening Messages.

Verification codes

A code from a text or an email shows up in the notch. One click copies it.

An AI that sees your screen

Ask Claude or ChatGPT from the notch, with a screenshot attached so it knows what you mean. One click turns that off.

Shazam

What's this song? The notch listens and names it.

A volume for every app

Turn the browser down and keep the music up, right from the notch.
